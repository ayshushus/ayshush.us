## Company Information
The company I worked at is called **Grant Thornton Bharat LLP**. Their website is https://www.grantthornton.in/. The company operates in the professional services industry, specifically within the accounting & tax & business advisory sector. The company is very large-sized and operates in over 150 countries with a US$8B+ revenue and has 80,000+ employees. "A member of Grant Thornton International Ltd, Grant Thornton Bharat is a leading professional services firm in the country. A truly Indian Firm with global connections - we work with businesses and government across industries and sectors, providing assurance, consulting, tax, risk and digital and technology transformation services.": https://www.grantthornton.in/en/about/.

## Position Information
I worked as a **Software Engineer** (intern). I worked for 4 months, from **May 2024 - Aug 2024** and worked specifically in the **dGTl (AI First Transformation)** team. I worked on representing data in various means and applying mathematical models and neural networks in various datasets. I learnt DBMS applications for millions of rows of data and this made me a much better engineer as I was forced to think about the greater impacts my work was having. The company is located in Nairobi, specifically in Kenya: https://www.innoviorafrica.com/#Contact.

## Project Context
The products I worked with were **[Oracle Netsuite](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/home.shtml?noredirect=T "Cloud ERP Systems") & [Microsoft Dynamics 365](https://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/dynamics-365 "Cloud ERP Systems")**, I was also exposed to a lot of the components in **Full Stack Software Engineering, DBMS & Edge Computing**. I learnt how to break concepts down to a degree that would enable stakeholders to properly understand the ideas as my target users & customers were **Onboarding new potential users along with Research & Development for MVP Edge Programming**. The main business purpose for my role was in the **ROI strategy, buyer transformation and applied technical systems for data storage**. The main goal of my internship was to **create a fully functional full stack MVP featuring Edge data storage & propagation**.
