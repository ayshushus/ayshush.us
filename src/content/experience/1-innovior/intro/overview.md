## Company Information
The company I worked at is called **INNOVIOR Africa Group**. Their website is https://www.innoviorafrica.com/. The company is in 2 industries, the first is Software and the second is IT Consulting. The company is mid-sized and operates in 5 countries with 15+ locations and has 51-200 employees. "INNOVIOR Africa is a multiple award-winning company that prides itself in Business Management Software implementation in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, Nigeria, and Ghana. We are the leading East and West Africa partner in the implementation of Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle NETSUITE, and Business Intelligence products to provide end-to-end ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning) and CRM (Customer Relationship Management) business solutions to our clients.": https://www.innoviorafrica.com/profile/about.

## Position Information
I worked as a **Software Engineer** (intern). I worked for 4 months, from **May 2023 - Aug 2023** and worked specifically in the **ERP Implementation & Consultancy** team. This way, I got to work both in the front-end and back-end of things. The front-end of things was where I took part in consultancy and talking to end-users in demoing the products we made as a company. The back-end of things was where I programmed full-stack systems and used my programming skills to develop a MVP-level mock web-application to present DBMS to C-suite executives in the board-level within INNOVIOR. The company is located in Nairobi, specifically in Kenya: https://www.innoviorafrica.com/#Contact.

## Project Context
The products I worked with were **[Oracle Netsuite](https://www.netsuite.com/portal/home.shtml?noredirect=T "Cloud ERP Systems") & [Microsoft Dynamics 365](https://www.microsoft.com/en-ca/dynamics-365 "Cloud ERP Systems")**, I was also exposed to a lot of the components in **Full Stack Software Engineering, DBMS & Edge Computing**. I learnt how to break concepts down to a degree that would enable stakeholders to properly understand the ideas as my target users & customers were **Onboarding new potential users along with Research & Development for MVP Edge Programming**. The main business purpose for my role was in the **ROI strategy and applied technical systems for data storage**. The main goal of my internship was to **create a fully functional full stack MVP featuring Edge data storage & propagation**.
