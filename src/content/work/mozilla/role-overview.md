## What Pontoon is

[Pontoon](https://pontoon.mozilla.org/) is Mozilla's open-source localization platform. It's where staff and a large volunteer community translate Mozilla products and websites into many locales. At a high level, it handles:

- **Strings** — the source text that needs translating.
- **Locales** — the target languages and their teams.
- **Translation memory** — past translations reused to keep things consistent.
- **Review workflows** — approving, rejecting, and discussing suggestions.
